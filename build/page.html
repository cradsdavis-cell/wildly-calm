Sound familiar?

Surrounded by people. Nobody who knows how you're really going.

  • Good, mate. Flat out.

    Run club, catch-ups, a birthday on the weekend. A full calendar, and the real stuff never comes up.

  • I'll sort it out myself.

    The lone wolf habit: never asking for help, never saying it out loud.

  • Therapy's not really me.

    Fair enough. A paddle, a campfire and a few other blokes is an easier place to start.

That's what Wildly Calm is: the easier place to start.

How a weekend works

Something hard first. Then the talk comes easier.

  1. Step 1:Do something hardA paddle, a hike, a cold river. It gets you out of your head and into your body.
  2. Step 2:Build the spaceFire, food, firewood, a few ground rules. Everyone pitches in.
  3. Step 3:Open up inside itDeep listening, sharing round the fire, and a few daft games so it never gets too heavy.
  4. Step 4:Take it homeThe group chat keeps going after. So do the friendships.

Our guiding principles

Community

Growing together and leaning on each other. There's more to life than being a lone wolf, and it's a relief to be around men without the judgement and ego.

Play

What's the point unless you're having fun? We make room for the boy inside who still wants to be silly, go on adventures and climb trees.

Authenticity

Dropping the act of being "one of the boys", leaving the toxic stuff behind and stepping into a healthier way of being a man.

Who we are

Peers, not counsellors.

Lockie Ransonco-founderKieran Maguireco-founderSam Davisco-founder

Wildly Calm is run by three mates. None of us is a therapist and we don't pretend to be. No guru at the front and no shouting at the sky. Just men who have sat in the circle ourselves and now help hold it. If someone needs more than a weekend can give, our job is to help him find it.
