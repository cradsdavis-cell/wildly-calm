Sound familiar?

Surrounded by people. Nobody who knows how you're really going.

  • Good, mate. Flat out.

    Run club, catch-ups, a birthday on the weekend. A full calendar, and the real stuff never comes up.

  • I'll sort it out myself.

    The lone wolf habit: never asking for help, never saying it out loud.

  • Therapy's not really me.

    Fair enough. A paddle, a campfire and a few other blokes is an easier place to start.

That's what Wildly Calm is: the easier place to start.

How a weekend works

Something hard first. Then the talk comes easier.

  1. Step 1Do something hard

    A paddle, a hike, a cold river. It gets you out of your head and into your body.

  2. Step 2Build the space

    Fire, food, firewood, a few ground rules. Everyone pitches in.

  3. Step 3Open up inside it

    Deep listening, sharing round the fire, and a few daft games so it never gets too heavy.

  4. Step 4Take it home

    The group chat keeps going after. So do the friendships.

Wildly Calm is run by three mates. None of us is a therapist and we don't pretend to be. We've sat in the circle ourselves and now help hold it. If someone needs more than a weekend can give, our job is to help him find it. Wildly Calm is a NSW not-for-profit.
