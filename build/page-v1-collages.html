@WildlyCalmRetreats

Nurture the man. Strengthen the boy.

Small outdoor retreats for men in their twenties and thirties. A few days somewhere wild, something hard to do together, and then the conversations that usually never happen.

Why we do this

Plenty of mates. Not many you'd ring at 11pm.

Ask a bloke how he's going and you'll get "good, mate, flat out". He's probably not lying. Morning swim, run club, two catch-ups and a birthday on the weekend. He's surrounded by people all week.

Ask him who he'd call to say what's really going on, and the list gets short. A lot of us run the lone wolf mission: do it on our own, never ask for help, "I just haven't found my tribe yet".

Life is good. But it's better shared. Most of us would never book a counselling session on a whim. Almost all of us would say yes to a three-day hike, a cold plunge or a night around a fire with a few other men. So that's the door we use.

How a weekend works

Something hard first. Then the talk comes easier.

  1. Step 1:Do something hardA long hike, a paddle, snow, a cold river. Together.
  2. Step 2:Build the spaceFire, food, firewood, ground rules. Everyone pitches in.
  3. Step 3:Open up inside itDeep listening, sharing round the fire, a few daft games.
  4. Step 4:The way backThe walk or drive out, when most of it lands.
  5. Step 5:Take it homeStaying in touch after, so it isn't just a good weekend.

Where we've been

It's not an adventure unless something goes wrong.

March 2025 · Where it started

Broughton Island

It started as a spontaneous weekend kayaking trip. Sea kayaks out to an island off Port Stephens, spearfishing for dinner, a tarp strung between the rocks. We came back with one big lesson: we loved it, and we wanted to keep making spaces like this for men.

October 2025

Camp Bunya

Kayaks on the river, yoga on the grass, sleeping bags round the fire. We came home knowing we wanted to go deeper, stay longer and get wilder.

February 2026 · East Gippsland, VIC

Croajingalong

A busload of Sydney men, three days of wilderness hiking and off-grid camping on the coast. Workshops between the walking, deep listening by the fire, and one afternoon where everyone painted the man they want to be. Grown men, paintbrushes, a lot of laughing, then some very honest pictures.

September 2026 · Crackenback, NSW

The Snowies

A smaller group in a cottage near Thredbo. A hike in the snow, sauna, ice bath. On the first night every man brought one thing he loves and talked about it by the fire for ten minutes. It sounds soft. Then you watch a quiet bloke light up about the thing he cares about.

"I knew I was in a slump, a proper downturn mentally before this trip. It wasn't till I was on the drive home that it truly hit me how important this trip was to the trajectory of my year."
Croajingalong participant

Our guiding principles

Community

Growing together and leaning on each other. There's more to life than being a lone wolf, and it's a relief to be around men without the judgement and ego.

Play

What's the point unless you're having fun? We make room for the boy inside who still wants to be silly, go on adventures and climb trees.

Authenticity

Dropping the act of being "one of the boys", leaving the toxic stuff behind and stepping into a healthier way of being a man.

Who we are

Peers, not counsellors.

Wildly Calm is run by three mates. None of us is a therapist and we don't pretend to be. No guru at the front and no shouting at the sky. Just men who have sat in the circle ourselves and now help hold it. If someone needs more than a weekend can give, our job is to help him find it.
