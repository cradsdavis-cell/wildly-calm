Barrington River

Three days canoeing the Barrington River. Friday 4 to Sunday 6 December 2026.

The plan

Three days on the Barrington River in canoes: paddling, camping by the water, fire at night and the circle.

Same shape as every Wildly Calm weekend. Something hard first, a space everyone builds together, and then the conversations that usually never happen.

When
Friday 4 to Sunday 6 December 2026
Where
Barrington River, NSW
What
Canoeing, camping, fire and circle
Who
Men in their twenties and thirties. Small group, on purpose.
Do I need to be fit or outdoorsy?

No. You don't need to be good at talking about feelings either. You just need to turn up.

Is this therapy?

No. It's peers, not counsellors. If you need more support, we'll help you find it.
