# Wildly Calm brand notes (for the site)

Sources read 2026-10-08: Rosie's branding guide (Drive, `260526_WILDLY CALM BRANDING GUIDE`, 12 pp) and the @wildlycalmretreats Instagram grid (14 posts).

## Visual
- Sand ground `#D7D2C6` (guide backgrounds, IG avatar).
- Olive panels with cream/white text and a white logo (IG "Our Guiding Principles" post). Site uses `#5E604A`.
- Amber `#F7AD19`: the guide's "highlight" hex, and the colour of the logo on IG photo posts. The guide's printed swatch looks rust (`#A65E39`), so the site uses rust on sand and amber on olive or photos. Still worth settling with Rosie before print.
- Logo: Oaxana wordmark with the hand-drawn mountains-and-sun symbol above it. Use the PNGs; the Oaxana text licence costs about USD 25.
- Type: Montserrat for body text, Gaegu for the handwritten layer; a typewriter face appears on IG collage captions.
- Imagery: film photography, torn-edge collages, white-bordered prints, hand-drawn white linework (contour swirls, trees, sun), and real men mid-activity.

## Voice (from IG captions)
- Casual, Aussie, playful, with honest questions: "It's not an adventure unless something goes wrong..", "Life is good. But, it's better shared.", "So.. you meditate? ... What about the other side of you?", "WE LOVE THIS SHIT".
- Retreat line: **Hike | Connect | Express**. Bio: **Nurture the man. Strengthen the boy.**
- Origin: "What first started as a spontaneous weekend kayaking trip" (Broughton Island, Mar 2025, captioned "Broughton Island - Men's Retreat").
- Guiding principles: **Community, Play, Authenticity** (the site paraphrases the post text).

## Practical
- Tickets go through Humanitix (the bio link still points at the Snowies event).
- Do not put a phone number on the site (Sam, 2026-10-08). Contact is email + Instagram.
